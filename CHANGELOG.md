# What's new

## 1.0.5 (6 November 2026)

- If you have more than one Xonar U1 connected, each of them now gets its proper name in Windows.

## 1.0.4 (31 October 2026)

- You can now check for updates from the panel. Click **Check for updates** at the bottom. If a newer version is out, click **Update** to install it, or just ignore it. The app only goes online when you click.

## 1.0.3 (31 October 2026)

- Installing now asks you to confirm only once, instead of twice.
- The app is more reliable. It no longer closes if its settings can't be saved for a moment, and it won't keep asking for admin approval if renaming the card isn't possible.
- Fixed the panel sometimes staying on screen after installing.

## 1.0.2 (31 October 2026)

- LED and knob settings are greyed out while the card isn't connected, so it's clear they can't be changed.
- Added support for the other version of the Xonar U1 (USB ID `0B05:1739`). It hasn't been tested yet, so please tell us whether it works for you.

## 1.0.1 (31 October 2026)

- The colour choice now looks the same as the other options in the panel.

## 1.0.0 (31 October 2026)

- First release.
- The volume knob changes the Windows volume, and pressing it mutes.
- Turn the LED off, or choose blue, red or purple, solid or blinking.
- Choose how sensitive the knob is.
- The card shows up as "ASUS Xonar U1" in Windows instead of "USB Advanced Audio Device".
- Starts with Windows, and installs or uninstalls in one click.
